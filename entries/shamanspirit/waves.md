A shaman who selects the waves spirit has a fluid grace that exhibits itself whenever she moves.
When she calls upon one of this spirit's abilities, floating orbs dance about her, sublimating
between icy crystals, misty vapors, and globules of water.

**Spirit Magic Spells**

**Info:** The shaman gains these spells at the listed spell levels.

**Spells:** Hydraulic push (1st)

**Spells:** Slipstream (2nd)

**Spells:** Water breathing (3rd)

**Spells:** Wall of ice (4th)

**Spells:** Geyser (5th)

**Spells:** Fluid form (6th)

**Spells:** Vortex (7th)

**Spells:** Seamantle (8th)

**Spells:** Tsunami (9th)

### Hexes

A shaman who chooses the Waves spirit can select from the following hexes.

**Beckoning Chill (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman causes one creature within 30 feet to become more susceptible to the sapping
powers of cold for 1 minute. When a creature takes cold damage while under this effect, it is
[entangled](ref/rule/entangled) for 1 round. If the creature takes cold damage while already
entangled by _beckoning chill,_ the duration of the entangled condition increases by 1 round.

**Special:** Once affected, the creature cannot be the target of this hex again for 24 hours.

**Crashing Waves (Su)**

**Flavor:** The force of a waves shaman's water spells can bring even the mightiest of foes to the
ground.

**Ability:** When the shaman casts a spell with the water descriptor, she does so at 1 caster level
higher. If that spell deals damage, the target must succeed at a Fortitude saving throw or be
knocked [prone](ref/rule/prone).

**At 8th level,** the shaman casts water spells at 2 caster levels higher.

**At 16th level,** her ability to knock creatures prone extends to any spell that deals damage.

**Fluid Magic (Su)**

**Flavor:** The shaman's magic is not constrained by the reservoirs of magic that hold others back.

**Ability:** She is able to prepare her spirit magic spells in her regular spell slots. If the
shaman changes her _wandering spirit,_ any prepared spirit magic spell belonging to that spirit
becomes an open spell slot.

**Mist's Shroud (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman touches a willing creature (including herself) and enshrouds that creature
in mist. This grants the creature [concealment](ref/rule/concealment) as the [blur](ref/spell/blur)
spell. The mist dissipates after it causes an attack to miss because of concealment or after 1
minute, whichever comes first.

**At 8th level,** the mist lasts for one additional attack.

**At 16th level,** the mist now fades after thwarting three attacks.

**Special:** A creature affected by this hex cannot be affected by it again for 24 hours.

**Water Sight (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman sees through fog and mist without penalty as long as there is enough light
to otherwise allow her to see normally.

**Usage:** 1 time/day per shaman level

**At 7th level,** she can use can use [scrying](ref/spell/scrying), using any calm pool of water
that's at least 1 foot in diameter as the sole focus.

**At 15th level,** this functions as [greater scrying](ref/spell/scrying-greater).

### Spirit Animal

**Spirit Animal**

**Flavor:** The skin of the shaman's spirit animal constantly distorts, much as a pond's surface
ripples when drops of water fall gently into it.

**Ability:** The animal gains [Mobility](ref/feat/mobility) as a bonus feat. The animal doesn't need
to meet the prerequisites for this feat. In addition, the animal can breathe underwater.

### Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Waves spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability.

**Wave Strike (Su)**

**Standard:** The shaman can perform a melee touch attack that drenches a creature and pushes it
away. The opponent takes an amount of nonlethal damage equal to 1d6 +
[half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) her shaman level and is pushed 5 feet directly away from the
shaman. This movement does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

**Usage:** 3 + Charisma modifier times/day

**At 11th level,** any melee weapon she wields is treated as if it had the
[quenching](ref/magic-enh/quenching) weapon special ability.

### Greater Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Waves spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the greater version of that spirit.

**Fluid Mastery (Su)**

**Ability:** The shaman gains a swim speed equal to her base land speed, as well as the ability to
breathe underwater.

**Standard:** In addition, she can unleash a torrent of ice and water from her hands in a 15-foot
cone. This torrent deals 1d4 points of cold damage per 2 shaman level she possesses, and pushes
affected creatures back 5 feet directly away from the shaman. A successful Reflex saving throw
halves the damage and negates the push.

The shaman can use this ability three times per day, but she must wait 1d4 rounds between each use.

### True Spirit Ability

A shaman who chooses the Waves spirit as her _spirit_ or _wandering spirit_ gains the following
ability upon having access to the true version of that spirit.

**Elemental Form (Su)**

**Standard:** The shaman assumes the form of a Huge (or smaller) water elemental, as
[elemental body IV](ref/spell/elemental-body-4) with a duration of 1 hour per level. The shaman can
use this ability once per day.

### Manifestation

**Manifestation**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Flavor:** The shaman becomes a master of cold and water.

**Ability:** She can also apply any one of the following feats to any cold or water spell she casts
without increasing the spell's level or casting time: [Enlarge Spell](ref/feat/enlarge-spell),
[Extend Spell](ref/feat/extend-spell), [Silent Spell](ref/feat/silent-spell), or
[Still Spell](ref/feat/still-spell). She doesn't need to possess these feats to use this ability.

The shaman gains cold resistance 30.

Primitive humans are less versatile than modern humans, shaped by daily survival in a harsh world.

## Physical Description

Primitive humans vary in height, skin tone, and body type. Most are about 6 inches shorter than
modern humans, have substantial body hair, appear unkempt, and have tan complexions from spending
much of their lives outdoors.

## Society

They live in small clans led by elders or powerful, warlike members. Clans usually span only a few
generations and frequently mix with nearby clans. Larger gatherings are usually short-lived unless
held together by a powerful or charismatic leader.

## Relations

Primitive humans fear most other races, and that fear can lead them to violence. They view elves and
goblins as threats; they distrust half-orcs and half-elves, though some clans accept them and may
treat them as superior.

## Alignment and Religion

Tribal laws are flexible, and concepts of good and evil are often unfamiliar. Some primitive humans
help others; some embrace destruction. Most have a simple religion centered on nature and local
spirits, making offerings and sacrifices to their deities.

## Adventurers

Some leave their clans to explore; others are exiled after conflicts or misdeeds. Outcasts may join
others to improve their chances of survival.

## Male Names

Arn, Bak, Farg, Gon, Merd, Oog, Tral, Ugg.

## Female Names

Ala, Desa, Fola, Ili, Hoda, Marg, Sus, Ula.

## Racial Traits

- **Ability Score** +2 to Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution.
- **Size** Medium, with no size-based bonuses or penalties.
- **Speed** 30 feet.
- **Frightened by Magic** When damaged or affected by a spell or supernatural ability with an
  obvious visual component, a primitive human is shaken for 1 round, subject to GM discretion. A
  primitive human who gains spellcasting from a class feature is unaffected.
- **Primitive Feat** At 1st level, choose Endurance, Fleet, Great Fortitude, Iron Will, Lightning
  Reflexes, or Toughness as a bonus feat.
- **Savage Skills** +2 racial bonus on Climb, Survival, and Swim checks.
- **Savage Surge** Once per day, as a free action before a check or attack roll, gain +2 on one
  Strength-based skill check, Strength check, or weapon damage roll.
- **Languages** Common. High-Intelligence characters may choose any languages except secret
  languages such as Druidic.

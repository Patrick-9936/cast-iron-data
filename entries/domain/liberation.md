**Granted Powers:** You are a spirit of freedom and a staunch foe against all who would enslave and
oppress.

**Liberation (Su)**

**Passive:** You have the ability to ignore impediments to your mobility. For as long as you have
rounds available, you can move normally regardless of magical effects that impede movement, as if
you were affected by [freedom of movement](ref/spell/freedom-of-movement). This effect occurs
automatically as soon as it applies.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per cleric level; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Freedom's Call (Su)**

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Standard:** You can emit a 30-foot aura of freedom. Allies within this aura are not affected by
the [confused](ref/misc/confused), grappled, pinned, [frightened](ref/misc/frightened),
[panicked](ref/misc/panicked), [paralyzed](ref/rule/paralyzed), or [shaken](ref/misc/shaken)
conditions. This aura only suppresses these effects, and they return once a creature leaves the aura
or when the aura ends, if applicable.

**Usage:** 1 round/day per cleric level; these rounds need not be consecutive

**Domain Spells**

1. remove fear
1. remove paralysis
1. remove curse
1. freedom of movement
1. break enchantment
1. greater dispel magic
1. refuge
1. mind blank
1. freedom

### Freedom Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 91

**Associated Deities:** Arshea, Black Butterfly, Desna, Eaqueo, Falayna, Feronia, Khepri, Llamolaek,
Lorris, Milani, Neshen, Scal, Shei, Sun Wukong, Taraksun, Vevelor, Xhasnaphar, Zaigasnar

**Liberty's Blessing (Sp)**

**Replaces:** The liberation power of the Liberation domain

**Standard:** You touch a willing creature, granting it a boon. A creature with this boon can, as a
swift action, make a saving throw against a single spell or effect it is suffering from that grants
a save. The DC of the saving throw is equal to the original DC of the spell or effect. If the saving
throw is successful, the effect ends. This boon lasts for 1 minute or until successfully used to
remove a spell or effect, whichever duration is shorter.

**Usage:** 3 + Wisdom modifier times/day

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**1st:** sanctuary

**5th:** plane shift

### Revolution Subdomain

**Sources:** Advanced Player's Guide pg 96

**Associated Deities:** Anogetz, Black Butterfly, Desna, Falayna, Khepri, Milani, Sun Wukong,
Taraksun, Vevelor, Xhasnaphar

**Powerful Persuader (Su)**

**Free:** When you make a Diplomacy or Intimidate check, you can roll twice and take the higher
result.

**Gained:** At 8th Level

**Replaces:** The freedom's call power of the Liberation domain

**Usage:** 1 time/day + 1 per 2 cleric levels beyond 8th

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**2nd:** enthrall

**6th:** symbol of persuasion

### Self-Realization Subdomain

**Sources:** Divine Anthology pg 26

**Prerequisites:** This subdomain requires the
[Acolyte of Apocrypha](ref/trait/acolyte-of-apocrypha) trait to access.

**Associated Domains:** Liberation, Strength

**Associated Deities:** Arshea, Falayna, Haagenti, Irori, Kurgess, Scal, Shei, Urgathoa

**Perfected Form (Su)**

**Flavor:** You maintain an unshakable sense of who you are, and no force can compel you to be
anything else.

**Immediate:** Once per day when you succeed at a saving throw against such an effect, you can gain
a surge of self-confidence that grants you a number of
[temporary hit points](ref/rule/temporary-hit-points) equal to your cleric level and a +2 morale
bonus on attack rolls, skill checks, and saving throws; both effects last for 1 minute.

**Passive:** You gain a sacred (if your patron is good or neutral) or profane (if your patron is
evil) bonus on saving throws against polymorph, petrification, and transmutation effects.
@B-5y1-cleric.

**Replaces:** The liberation power of the Liberation domain

**Replacement Domain Spells**

**4th:** paragon surge (always matches your actual race)

**6th:** primal scream

**7th:** transformation

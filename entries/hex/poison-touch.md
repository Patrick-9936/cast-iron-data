**Poison Touch (Ex)**

**Ability:** The witch empowers herself or an ally within 30 feet with poisonous nails. The affected
creature gains a [claw attack](ref/umr/natural-attacks) as a secondary attack that deals 1d3 points
of damage (1d2 for Small creatures) and has the following poison ability: Witch Toxin: Claw-injury;
save Fort DC 10 + half the witch's level + the witch's Intelligence modifier; frequency 1/round for
6 rounds; effect 1d2 Str; cure 1 save.

The creature gains this claw attack for a number of minutes equal to the witch's level. If the
target already has a claw attack, that attack gains the poison ability listed above, but the DC is 1
higher.

**Special:** Once a creature has benefited from this hex, it cannot benefit from it again for 24
hours.

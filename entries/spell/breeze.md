::: statblock
title = "Spell statistics"
School: evocation \[air\]
Level: 0: Sorcerer, Wizard
Casting time: 1 standard action
Components: V, S, M (a miniature fan)
Range: close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Duration: 1 hour (D)
Saving throw: Will negates (harmless)
Spell resistance: yes
Target: one creature or object
:::

A light wind blows against the target from a direction of your choice. The breeze grants the target
a +2 bonus on saves against very hot conditions, severe heat, breath weapons, and saves against
cloud vapors and gases (such as cloudkill, stinking cloud, and inhaled poisons). This spell does not
function without air or underwater. Only one breeze can be active at a time; casting another
dismisses the previous casting.

**Note:** This is a rare cantrip. Spellcasters that gain access to all 0-level spells at 1st level
do not gain access to rare cantrips. A spellcaster gains access to rare cantrips only by uncovering
their secrets in some other way. Some are guarded by jealous mages, while others are lost in missing
libraries or molder on forgotten scrolls.

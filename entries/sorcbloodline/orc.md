The rage of your ancestors burns within you, and the taint of savage [orc](ref/monster/orc) blood
flows through your veins. Your anger is never far from the surface, giving you strength and driving
you to seek greater power.

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** You gain the [orc](ref/subtype/orc) subtype, including
[darkvision](ref/rule/darkvision) 60 feet and [light sensitivity](ref/umr/light-sensitivity). If you
already have darkvision, its range increases to 90 feet.

**Passive:** Whenever you cast a spell that deals damage, that spell deals +1 point of damage per
die rolled.

### Bloodline Powers

You are heir to the bestial power of the great orc warlords of the past. You feel their rage in your
blood, and must take care lest the urge to despoil and destroy consumes you.

**Touch of Rage (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can touch a creature, giving it a morale bonus on attack rolls, damage rolls, and
Will saving throws equal to [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your sorcerer level (minimum
1) for 1 round.

**Usage:** 3 + Charisma modifier times/day

**Fearless (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain a +4 bonus on saving throws made against fear and a +1 natural armor bonus.

**At 9th level,** you lose your light sensitivity, gain immunity to fear, and your natural armor
bonus increases to +2.

**Strength of the Beast (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Passive:** You gain a +2 inherent bonus to your Strength.

**At 13th level,** this bonus increases to +4.

**At 17th level,** this bonus increases to +6.

**Power of Giants (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Standard:** You grow to Large size. At this size you gain a +6 size bonus to Strength, a -2
penalty to Dexterity, a +4 size bonus to Constitution, and a +4 natural armor bonus. You may return
to your normal size as a **standard action**.

**Special:** You may remain in this size for up to 1 minute per character level per day; this
duration does not need to be consecutive, but it must be used in 1 minute increments.

**Warlord Reborn (Su)**

**Ability:** Once per day, you can cast _[transformation](ref/spell/transformation)_ as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities) using your sorcerer level as your caster level.

**Flavor:** You become a true orc warlord of legend.

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Passive:** You gain immunity to fire and DR 5/-.

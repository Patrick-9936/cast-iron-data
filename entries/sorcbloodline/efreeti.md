You were born with the power of fire genies, and the magic of the
[efreet](ref/monster/genie-efreeti) is strong in you.

**Gained class skills:** [Knowledge (planes)](ref/skill/knowledge-planes)

**Bonus Spells by Sorcerer Level**

**Spells:** Enlarge person (3rd)

**Spells:** Scorching ray (5th)

**Spells:** Fireball (7th)

**Spells:** Wall of fire (9th)

**Spells:** Persistent image (11th)

**Spells:** Planar binding (13th)

**Spells:** Plane shift (15th)

**Spells:** Giant form II (17th)

**Spells:** Wish (19th)

**Bonus Feats:** [Dodge](ref/feat/dodge), [Empower Spell](ref/feat/empower-spell),
[Great Fortitude](ref/feat/great-fortitude), [Improved Initiative](ref/feat/improved-initiative),
[Lightning Reflexes](ref/feat/lightning-reflexes), [Power Attack](ref/feat/power-attack),
[Skill Focus](ref/feat/skill-focus) (Knowledge \[planes\]),
[Weapon Finesse](ref/feat/weapon-finesse)

**Bloodline Arcana**

**Ability:** Whenever you cast a spell that deals energy damage, you can change the type of damage
to fire. This also changes the spell's descriptors to match this energy type.

### Bloodline Powers

Like an efreeti, you have both natural power over air and several other genie-based abilities.

**Fire Ray (Sp)**

**Gained:** At 1st Level

**Standard:** You can unleash an elemental ray, targeting any foe within 30 feet as a ranged touch
attack. This ray deals fire damage equal to 1d6 + [half](ref/misc/fractions-and-rounding) of your
sorcerer level.

**Usage:** 3 + Charisma modifier times/day

**Elemental Resistance (Ex)**

**Gained:** At 3rd Level

**Passive:** You gain fire [resistance](ref/umr/resistance) 10.

**At 9th level,** your fire resistance increases to 20.

**Efreeti Form (Su)**

**Gained:** At 9th Level

**Ability:** You gain the ability to assume the form of an efreeti once per day. This acts like the
spell [giant form I](ref/spell/giant-form-1), except you only use it to become an efreeti and its
duration is 1 round per level. While in this form, you also gain an efreeti's
_[heat](ref/carried/bestiary/rules/universal-monster-rules/heat)_ ability.

**Heat**

**Passive:** An efreeti's body deals 1d6 points of fire damage whenever it hits in melee, or in each
round it [grapples](ref/rule/grapple).

**Elemental Movement (Su)**

**Gained:** At 15th Level

**Passive:** Your base speed increases by +30 feet.

**Power of the Efreet (Su)**

**Gained:** At 20th Level

**Flavor:** You gain the power of genies to grant wishes.

**Ability:** Once per day, you can cast [limited wish](ref/spell/limited-wish) as a
[spell-like ability](ref/umr/spell-like-abilities). Such wishes must begin with the words "I wish,"
and cannot duplicate a wish you have granted within the past 24 hours. If you use this ability to
duplicate a spell with a costly material component, you must provide that component.

You also become immune to fire damage.

You can use _[plane shift](ref/spell/plane-shift)_ once per day to travel to or from the Plane of
Fire.
